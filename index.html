Our Story

Welcome to Cozy Loops! What started as a love for creating tiny crochet creatures has grown into a full collection of adorable amigurumi friends.

Every character is handmade using the Japanese art of amigurumi - creating small, stuffed yarn creatures. We use premium acrylic and cotton yarns, safety eyes, and hypoallergenic stuffing to ensure each friend is safe, soft, and built to last.

Whether you're looking for a unique gift, a nursery companion, or just a cute desk buddy, each amigurumi is made with love and comes with its own special personality!

📝 Pattern Policy: Crochet patterns are available for $10 each, but must be purchased together with at least one finished amigurumi item. This ensures you have a reference piece while learning!
